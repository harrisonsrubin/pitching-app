# P3 scorecard: projection

Generated 2026-11-21. Base seasons 2021–2025, each fit on data through that season only and scored on the next season (or the rest of that season).
Next season: MLB pitchers with 50+ IP in both seasons, weighted by the smaller (n = 1,062). RA9 is park-adjusted; benchmarks pass through a linear fit cross-validated by pitcher, and so does the projection.

## Success criteria

- PASS — Projection beats Marcel on next-season RA9 RMSE (1.023 vs 1.057)
- FAIL — Projection beats Pitching+ on next-season RA9 RMSE (1.023 vs 1.015)
- FAIL — Projection adds more than Pitching+ on top of prior RA9 (Δ RMSE -0.0413 vs -0.0520)
- PASS — Projection beats Marcel + window RA9 on rest-of-season RA9 RMSE after 100 pitches (1.151 vs 1.189)
- PASS — Projection beats Marcel + window RA9 on rest-of-season RA9 RMSE after 250 pitches (1.164 vs 1.205)
- PASS — Projection beats Marcel + window RA9 on rest-of-season RA9 RMSE after 500 pitches (1.161 vs 1.192)
- PASS — 90% season ranges cover 85–95% of next-season ERAs (93.8%, qualified pitchers)
- PASS — 90% season ERA ranges cover 85–95% for every pitcher with 20+ IP next season (93.0%)
- PASS — Innings projection beats Marcel playing time (RMSE 31.4 vs 37.2 IP)
- PASS — WAR projection beats Marcel playing time (RMSE 0.84 vs 0.86)
- FAIL — 90% innings ranges cover 85–95% (95.4%)
- PASS — 90% WAR ranges cover 85–95% (88.1%)

## What is and is not held out

The projection never sees the season it is scored on. The pitch models and grade anchors are fixed P1 models trained on MLB 2023–2026; each pitcher's pitches are scored by a model that never saw him, but the league-level fit has seen the scored seasons. That overlap is in the pitch-quality inputs, not in any pitcher's results.
